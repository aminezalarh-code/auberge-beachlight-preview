Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Who is responsible

Auberge Beachlight, Imsouane, Morocco, is responsible for the processing of your personal data. Contact: contact@aubergebeachlight.com.

What we collect

Only what you send us in the booking request form or by WhatsApp: your name, email, phone number, travel dates, number of guests, room preference, surf level, extras and your message.

Why we use it

To answer your request, confirm your booking, prepare your stay and contact you about it. We never sell your data and never use it for advertising without your consent.

Where it is stored

Booking requests are saved only on our own web server and on the auberge’s computer. They are not shared with any booking platform or marketing service. If you choose to send your request on WhatsApp, it is also processed by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep booking requests for as long as needed to manage your stay and our legal obligations, and at most 3 years after your last contact with us.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are served from our own server and the surf report data is fetched by our server, so no personal data is sent to these providers when you browse.

The map on the home page is loaded from OpenStreetMap (openstreetmap.org), which receives your IP address to display it and does not set advertising cookies. The Google Maps and Waze buttons only open those services if you click them.

Your rights

Under Moroccan Law 09-08 on the protection of personal data and, for visitors from the European Union, the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use. Write to us at contact@aubergebeachlight.com. You can also contact the CNDP (www.cndp.ma) in Morocco or your national data protection authority.
