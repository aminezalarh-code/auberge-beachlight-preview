Terms & conditions

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Booking requests

The form on this website sends a booking request: it is not a confirmed booking and no payment is taken. We reply by email or WhatsApp with availability and the price. The person booking must be at least 18 years old and is responsible for the whole group.

Prices

Prices are shown in Moroccan dirhams (MAD) per room per night, or per bed per night in the dorms, breakfast included. Surf classes (350 MAD) and yoga classes (150 MAD) are per person. The final price is the one written in your confirmation.

Deposit and payment

To confirm your booking we ask for a deposit of 30% of the stay, within 3 days of our offer; after that the dates are released. Your booking is confirmed when we receive the deposit and send you a written confirmation.

For bookings made less than 14 days before arrival, the full amount may be requested to confirm.

The balance is paid on arrival, by card (Visa, Mastercard) or in cash (Moroccan dirhams or euros). Extras (classes, transfers, trips) are paid on site.

Arrival and departure

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 11:00. The minimum stay is 2 nights. Please tell us your arrival time in advance.

House rules

Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 08:00. Pets, smoking, alcohol and drugs are not allowed in the auberge. Wildflower and Rose are women-only dorms. Visitors who are not staying at the auberge must be announced at reception.

Respectful behaviour is expected towards other guests and the team. Serious breaches of these rules may end the stay without refund.

Damage and belongings

Guests are responsible for any damage they cause to the rooms, the house or the equipment (including surfboards) and must report it straight away. Please look after your valuables: the auberge is not responsible for lost or stolen items.

Surf, yoga and activities

Surf classes, trips and activities are run by local instructors and partners. Surfing and outdoor activities involve risks: you must be able to swim, follow the instructors’ advice and respect your level. Waves and weather are never guaranteed; when conditions are not right, sessions are moved or replaced, not refunded.

Insurance

We strongly recommend travel insurance covering health, cancellation and surfing. The auberge is not liable for injuries or accidents during activities, except where the law provides otherwise.

Cancellation

See our cancellation policy.

Applicable law

These terms are governed by Moroccan law. In case of dispute, we will first look for a friendly solution; failing that, the competent courts of Agadir will have jurisdiction.
