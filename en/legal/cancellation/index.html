Cancellation policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Deposit

Your booking is confirmed with a deposit of 30% of the stay. The balance is paid on arrival.

Cancelling your stay

More than 14 days before arrival: your deposit is refunded in full.

14 days or less before arrival: the deposit is kept.

For stays of 28 nights or more, and for groups of 3 rooms or more, the deadline is 30 days before arrival instead of 14.

Changing your dates

More than 14 days before arrival, you can move your stay once for free, subject to availability: your deposit is transferred to the new dates. Later changes are treated as a cancellation.

No-show and early departure

If you do not arrive without telling us, the deposit is kept. Nights not used because you leave earlier than planned are not refunded.

Waves and weather

Waves and weather are not guaranteed and are not a reason for a refund. When conditions are not right, surf sessions are moved or replaced.

How to cancel

Write to us by email (contact@aubergebeachlight.com) or WhatsApp (+212 660 749 129). The date of your message counts as the cancellation date, and the cancellation is final once we confirm it in writing. Refunds are made with the payment method you used, within 14 days.

If we have to cancel

If we cannot welcome you for a reason on our side, we refund everything you paid, or offer you new dates if you prefer.

In case of force majeure (natural disaster, border closure, official travel restrictions…), we offer a credit valid 12 months or a refund of the deposit. A missed or cancelled flight is not force majeure: travel insurance is recommended.
