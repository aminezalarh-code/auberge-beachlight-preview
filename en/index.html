Imsouane
travel guide

Imsouane beach

The beach of Imsouane (plage d’Imsouane) curves around a sheltered bay with a small fishing harbour. Calm water in the morning, long waves all day.

Surf season

Autumn to spring (roughly September to April) brings the most consistent Atlantic swell. Summer waves are smaller, which is ideal for beginners. Check our live surf report or Windguru before you come.

Imsouane weather

Sunny and mild all year: days around 18–22 °C in winter and 24–28 °C in summer, with cool evenings. The sea is about 20 °C in autumn. Bring a hoodie for the rooftop.

Getting here

Imsouane is on the coast road between Agadir (about 1 h 30) and Essaouira (about 1 h 30). Taghazout is about 1 h away and Marrakech about 4 h. Ask us about a pickup.

Is Imsouane good for beginner surfers?+

Yes. Imsouane Bay has long, slow, soft waves that are among the most beginner-friendly in Morocco. We book surf classes for 350 MAD per person.

Yes, three dorms with their own bathroom: Wildflower (6 beds) and Rose (4 beds) are women’s dorms, and Basil has 6 beds. From 250 MAD per bed with breakfast.

What are the check-in and check-out times?+

Check-in from 14:00, check-out by 11:00. The minimum stay is 2 nights.

When is the best surf season in Imsouane?+

Autumn to spring (roughly September to April) for the most consistent waves. Summer brings smaller waves, perfect for first lessons.

How do I get from Agadir or Taghazout to Imsouane?+

By car or taxi along the coast road: about 1 h 30 from Agadir and about 1 h from Taghazout. Ask us about a pickup.

Sunny and mild all year, with cooler evenings and a fresh Atlantic breeze. The sea is around 20 °C in autumn.
