Travelling solo? Our three dorms each have their own bathroom and solid wooden bunk beds. Wildflower (6 beds) and Rose (4 beds) are women-only dorms, a calm and safe choice for solo female surfers, and Basil (6 beds) is our men’s dorm. Breakfast is included and the beach is just around the corner.

Check-in from 14:00 · check-out by 11:00 · minimum stay 2 nights
